# Physical AI system design

Canonical: https://codeatoms.ai/physical-ai-system-design/
License: CC BY 4.0
Publisher: CodeNinja Atoms (https://codeatoms.ai)

Physical AI system design is the engineering of a system that senses a physical operation, reasons over it as typed objects and acts through a named person. Its decisions are what to sense, where inference runs (the edge, the site or one in-country node), the object model, the open-weight model choice, the compute sized from those models' weights, the write paths back into the operator's systems and human control. CodeNinja Atoms has published 44 worked examples of physical AI system design across 12 sectors in the United States (19), Saudi Arabia (13), Pakistan (11), Germany (1), each free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

## The six decisions in a physical AI system design

| Decision | What the published designs do |
|---|---|
| 1. Sensing and sources | What the system reads: the operator's existing systems of record, read only, plus the cameras, detectors and instruments the operation needs. 43 of 44 designs name their sources on the first page of the paper; every design names them in the body. |
| 2. Where inference runs | 6 of 44 designs state an edge or boundary tier in their At a glance table. By compute pattern: 28 frontier reasoning node, 7 one inference card, 9 no new compute; in every design inference stays in the operator's country. |
| 3. The object model | 643 typed objects and 560 typed links across the 44 designs, each published as hyper-ontology/1 JSON. |
| 4. Open-weight model choice | 127 model choices in all, each with its licence and the reason it was picked; no closed model in any serving path. |
| 5. Sizing from the weights | 44 of 44 designs state their compute and 44 of 44 their three-year cost on the first page, sized from the models' own weights, from no new hardware to an eight-GPU node. |
| 6. Write paths and human control | 44 of 44 designs name their write paths (how a decision is written into the owning system) and 44 of 44 name the person who approves each action that changes the physical world. |

## The hardware in a physical AI system design

Hardware is chosen after the models and sized from their weights. The 44 designs by compute pattern:

| Compute pattern | What it is | Designs |
|---|---|---|
| Frontier reasoning node | eight 141 GB HBM-class GPUs holding an open-weight frontier model at FP8, owned or hosted in the country | 28 |
| One inference card | 48 GB PCIe inference cards, from one card to one eight-card server, at the site or in the country; no frontier model | 7 |
| No new compute | the design runs on servers or software the operator already owns; nothing to buy | 9 |
