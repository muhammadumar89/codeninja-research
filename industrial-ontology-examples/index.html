Ontology · Examples

Industrial ontology examples

An industrial ontology is a typed model of a physical operation: its assets, records, events and people as objects, each with properties, status values and named links to the others, anchored in the systems that already hold the data. Below are 21 published industrial ontology examples, 288 objects and 268 links in all, one per real operation across 9 sectors. Each downloads as hyper-ontology/1 JSON under CC BY 4.0.

An industrial ontology example, read object by object

Take one object from Hangar Form Cell (aviation and aerospace manufacturing, United States): Formed structural part, an asset. Its properties are what the operation records about it: Part number, Serial number, Alloy and temper, Sheet thickness, As-built deviation to CAD, Formability verdict. Its links are what make this an ontology rather than a table: each one is typed and points at another object in the same model.

  • formed from Sheet blank
  • formed in Dieless cell
  • defined by Part CAD model
  • judged by Formability analysis
  • verified by In-process metrology scan
  • traced by Force trace
  • ordered under Work order
