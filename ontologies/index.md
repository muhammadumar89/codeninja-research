# Ontologies

Canonical: https://codeatoms.ai/ontologies/
License: CC BY 4.0
Publisher: CodeNinja Atoms (https://codeatoms.ai)

An ontology is a typed model of a domain: the kinds of things in it, the properties each one has, and the named links between them. Ontologies let people and AI agents work from one shared model instead of many tables. In an industrial operation, an ontology names each asset, record, event, site and person the operation holds, anchors each one in the system that stores its data, and joins them by typed links. CodeNinja Atoms has published 44 ontologies of real operations across 12 sectors in the United States (19), Saudi Arabia (13), Pakistan (11), Germany (1): 643 objects and 560 links, each downloadable as hyper-ontology/1 JSON under CC BY 4.0.

## What an ontology contains

| Kind | What it is | Objects |
|---|---|---|
| record | what the operation writes down: work orders, tickets, findings, calibrations | 180 |
| asset | equipment and other physical things the operation owns and maintains | 155 |
| event | something that happens at a time: an alarm, a fault, a mission | 90 |
| actor | a role that acts on the operation: a desk, a crew, an officer | 47 |
| site | a place the operation runs: a station, a field, a centre | 46 |
| measure | a reading, sample or score taken from the operation | 38 |
| document | a report, letter or dataset the operation keeps | 35 |
| person | a named job a person holds: the one who reads, decides or approves | 20 |
| system | a software or control system the model names as an object in its own right | 16 |
| material | stock that moves through the operation: parts, wafers, containers | 15 |
