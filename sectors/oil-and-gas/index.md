# Physical AI for oil and gas: questions answered

Canonical: https://codeatoms.ai/sectors/oil-and-gas/
License: CC BY 4.0
Publisher: CodeNinja Atoms (https://codeatoms.ai)

Wells, terminals and plants hold safety, inventory and process data in systems that were never joined, often on sites where the data may not leave the country.

## What does a physical AI system for oil and gas look like?

A complete physical AI design for oil and gas names what to sense, which existing systems to join, the object model that joins them, the models and hardware, the three-year cost and the person who approves every action. CodeNinja Atoms has published 4 such reference architectures for oil and gas, each free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Sovereign HSE Watch (Pakistan): An open reference architecture for predicting HSE incidents at an oil and gas operator in Pakistan, on the operator's own hardware, with no data leaving the country and no third-party AI service in the serving path. Loop Integrity Watch (Pakistan): field hardware that rebuilds the signal path on three Modbus loops, and a governed inventory ontology that catches a flatlined or swapped radar gauge reading before the central inventory picture misleads anyone. Corridor Guard (Pakistan): a validation-first study that digitises an operator's scanned pipeline records, verifies the corridor in the field, calibrates a direct current interference model in validated software and hands the re-runnable model over on the operator's own hardware, so every safe distance ships with its evidence. Pipeline Integrity Watch (United States): An open reference architecture for system design in physical AI. A single on-premises model of a natural gas transmission estate in the United States: passive taps watch the OT network at every station and in the gas control centers, open-weight models watch compressor health and leak indicators at the station, and a control room assistant explains each alert and drafts a response for a pipeline controller who decides every action. The security directive evidence generates itself from the monitoring.

## Which AI models can an oil and gas operator run on its own hardware?

Each published oil and gas design names its models and why, and every model is open-weight or no model is used at all, so the operator can run it on hardware it owns. Sovereign HSE Watch: 6 self-hosted open-weight models, including GLM 5.3 (reasoning), Chronos-2 (forecasting), RF-DETR (vision), BGE-M3 (retrieval in English and Urdu) and PaddleOCR-VL 1.6 (scans). Loop Integrity Watch: None: every integrity check (stuck value, swap, distortion) is deterministic. Corridor Guard: . Pipeline Integrity Watch: Mistral Large 3 675B (Apache-2.0) for the control room assistant, the compliance agent and the work surface; Chronos-2 (Apache-2.0) for telemetry forecasting with residual anomaly detection; RF-DETR Apache sizes for station camera scene detection; BGE-M3 (MIT) for retrieval.

## How much compute and hardware does AI in oil and gas need?

The compute follows from the models: the published oil and gas designs size it as follows, from no new hardware to a full GPU node. Sovereign HSE Watch, frontier compute: One node of eight 141 GB HBM-class GPUs holds GLM 5.3 at FP8 (753 GB of weights, 904 GB with headroom). Sovereign HSE Watch, the hard dependency: 141 GB-class accelerators need a US export licence for Pakistan (Country Group D:4); the rollout's first gate confirms installed hardware first. Loop Integrity Watch, compute: Two containers on the operator's own site application server, inside its OT boundary. Loop Integrity Watch, field scope: 13 fuel tanks on 3 Modbus RTU loops; 3 booster installations engineered from a measured signal survey inside explosion proof junction boxes. Corridor Guard, compute: One server-class host of the 48 GB PCIe inference GPU class inside the operator's estate; no field compute, no cloud, nothing crosses the boundary. Pipeline Integrity Watch, compute: At each station, volume production edge modules in 7W to 60W envelopes inside hazardous area enclosures, detecting through backhaul loss; centrally, one node of eight 141 GB HBM-class GPUs in the gas control center.

## Is it cheaper to own AI hardware or rent cloud GPUs in oil and gas?

Each oil and gas design prices three years of ownership in its Appendix A, with every price cited, against renting the same capacity from a cloud region at its deepest three-year commitment where hardware is bought. Sovereign HSE Watch: three-year cost, owned, About 670,000 US dollars with support and power at Pakistan's industrial tariff; three-year cost, rented, 1.1 to 2.8 million US dollars for the same GPUs in the nearest cloud region; no hyperscaler runs a region in Pakistan. Loop Integrity Watch: three-year cost, No compute to price; the field equipment is priced by OEM quotation against the survey (Appendix A). Corridor Guard: three-year cost, About 19,500 US dollars to own the host over three years (18,700 to 20,200), about four fifths of the deepest three-year AWS commitment for one L40S card, which could not keep the data in Pakistan in any case (Appendix A). Pipeline Integrity Watch: three-year cost, About 465,000 US dollars to own the control center node for three years (432,000 to 485,000), about three quarters of the 625,000 the deepest AWS commitment in US East costs and well under the 1.66 million on demand; the station modules, enclosures, cameras, diodes and sensors are priced by quotation (Appendix A).

## What ontology or object model does an oil and gas AI system need?

The object model is the part that makes the system an ontology rather than a pipeline: typed objects for the things in the oil and gas operation, with properties, status values and typed links. Every published design ships its object model as hyper-ontology/1 JSON that loads into Hyper Ontology. Sovereign HSE Watch: Operating Facility / Site, HSE Equipment, HSE Incident, Near Miss Report, Corrective Action, Inspection / Audit Record, HSE Document, Sensor Reading, Anomaly Event, Agent Recommendation, HSE Person, HSE Role. Loop Integrity Watch: Fuel Tank, Radar Tank Gauge (RTG), RTG Communication Loop, Modbus Booster (Repeater), Explosion Proof Junction Box, Gauge Reading, Data Quality Event, Central Inventory Picture, Loop Wiring As-Built, Service Order, Warranty and Support Record, OEM Authorization Letter, HSE Work Permit, Instrumentation Technician, Location Engineer. Corridor Guard: 8-inch Line, 10-inch Loop Line, Original Anode Bed, TEG Unit, Anode 1 (MP-3.40), Anode 2 (MP-6.97), Insulating Joints PFIJ, Electrode Station, HVDC Transmission System, CP Test Point, Cathodic Protection System, Soil Investigation Report, Detail of Available Data for Assessment, DC Interference Assessment, Mitigation Measure. Pipeline Integrity Watch: Compressor Station, Meter Station, Pipeline Segment, Compressor Unit, OT Device, Pipeline Controller, Alarm, Leak or Abnormal Operation Event, Remote Access Session, Work Order, Security Directive Control, Historian, CMMS, Hazardous Area Classification.

## Who approves the decisions an AI system makes in oil and gas?

In every published oil and gas design a named person makes the decision that changes the physical world; the system prepares it. Sovereign HSE Watch: Every recommendation is approved or rejected by a named person; nothing executes on equipment. Loop Integrity Watch: Every data quality event is acknowledged and resolved by a named person; the location engineer signs acceptance. Corridor Guard: A named corrosion control engineer closes every discrepancy and accepts every assessment; no value enters the register without provenance and no safe distance ships without its evidence. Pipeline Integrity Watch: The system never writes to a field device and shutdown stays with the certified control paths; a hardware one-way diode is the only way out of each station OT zone; every decision-influencing model runs in shadow mode before any controller-facing alert, and a named controller confirms or clears every leak event.
