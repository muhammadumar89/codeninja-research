What does a physical AI system for maritime and ports look like?

A complete physical AI design for maritime and ports names what to sense, which existing systems to join, the object model that joins them, the models and hardware, the three-year cost and the person who approves every action. CodeNinja Atoms has published 2 such reference architectures for maritime and ports, each free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

Is it cheaper to own AI hardware or rent cloud GPUs in maritime and ports?
