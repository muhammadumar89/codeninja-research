What does a physical AI system for energy and utilities look like?

A complete physical AI design for energy and utilities names what to sense, which existing systems to join, the object model that joins them, the models and hardware, the three-year cost and the person who approves every action. CodeNinja Atoms has published 8 such reference architectures for energy and utilities, each free to reuse under CC BY 4.0.

Who approves the decisions an AI system makes in energy and utilities?

In every published energy and utilities design a named person makes the decision that changes the physical world; the system prepares it.
