# Physical AI for agriculture and earth observation: questions answered

Canonical: https://codeatoms.ai/sectors/agriculture-and-earth-observation/
License: CC BY 4.0
Publisher: CodeNinja Atoms (https://codeatoms.ai)

Farms, irrigation and land use are watched by sensors, satellites and aircraft, but the readings, the licences and the field records usually sit in separate systems.

## What does a physical AI system for agriculture and earth observation look like?

A complete physical AI design for agriculture and earth observation names what to sense, which existing systems to join, the object model that joins them, the models and hardware, the three-year cost and the person who approves every action. CodeNinja Atoms has published 4 such reference architectures for agriculture and earth observation, each free to reuse under CC BY 4.0. Field Ledger (Pakistan): one dashboard that joins a farm's live sensor feeds, its historical datasets and a big data and analytics repository into one object model, so any farm, crop cycle or season can be drilled into, exported and reported on under role-based access. Baseline (United States): one summer flight of 3-inch 4-band orthoimagery and USGS Quality Level 1 LiDAR, with every tile, point cloud and elevation model bound into a site ontology, so the next season starts from a comparison instead of rediscovery. Fodder Watch (Saudi Arabia): an 18-month earth observation service that screens every parcel for restricted green fodder and cultivation beyond the licensed area, checks each detection against the holding's licence, and turns the confirmed ones into case files an inspector can act on. A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan (Pakistan): An open reference architecture for a self-improving AI system in physical AI, built for a provincial irrigation department in Pakistan that takes its share of river water at barrages and head regulators and delivers it through a main canal, distributaries and minors to outlets farmers share by rotation, where owning the inference hardware that runs it costs about 636,000 US dollars over three years. When a head regulator reading drops below its indent for three days at peak demand, the design joins the telemetry, the indent and release ledgers, the rotation schedule, the gauge reader's diary, the complaint register and the revenue crop records into one entitlement versus delivery argument, so the divisional engineer sees which reach is silted, which branch is over drawing and which gate adjustment cured the matching shortfall last season. Every model runs on the department's own hardware inside Pakistan under the inter provincial water accord, canal and drainage law, the national data rules for farmer records and export control on the GPU node.

## Which AI models can an agriculture and earth observation operator run on its own hardware?

Each published agriculture and earth observation design names its models and why, and every model is open-weight or no model is used at all, so the operator can run it on hardware it owns. Field Ledger: None: the analytics are deterministic aggregations, drill-down and reporting. Baseline: None: vegetation analysis stays with the operator's own analysts. Fodder Watch: RF-DETR fine-tuned per region and Chronos-2 zero-shot, both Apache-2.0, about 0.55 GB of weights together. A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan: Frontier class open weights for the three drafting agents (shortfall, indent and dispute) and the command centre, self hosted for sovereignty; the operator names GLM 5.3 and the plan holds its FP8 arithmetic, but no weights are committed until the register verifies a model live; embedding, OCR and forecasting model classes for retrieval, handwritten Urdu and English ledgers and shortfall detection; no third party model service, nothing trained into weights.

## How much compute and hardware does AI in agriculture and earth observation need?

The compute follows from the models: the published agriculture and earth observation designs size it as follows, from no new hardware to a full GPU node. Field Ledger, stack: Open-source PostgreSQL with PostGIS on the operator's own servers; source code and full intellectual property handed over. Baseline, ground: The operator's ArcGIS Enterprise; nothing runs in a cloud the operator does not control. Baseline, acquisition: One manned flight within a week either side of 1 July; 3-inch 4-band (red, green, blue, near infrared) orthoimagery and Quality Level 1 LiDAR at a minimum of 8 pulses per square meter. Fodder Watch, compute: One 48 GB L40S-class inference node inside the Kingdom; the card class needs a US export licence. A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan, compute: One node of eight 141 GB HBM cards (about 1,128 GB) in the department's circle server room, the held arithmetic for about 756 GB of FP8 weights, beside a 48 GB PCIe class site server for the small model tier; no edge tier, nothing served at a canal head. The node sits above the US advanced computing export threshold for a D:2, D:3 and D:4 destination, so the export licence determination runs first in phase one with an in country procurement route or colocation fallback named before any order.

## Is it cheaper to own AI hardware or rent cloud GPUs in agriculture and earth observation?

Each agriculture and earth observation design prices three years of ownership in its Appendix A, with every price cited, against renting the same capacity from a cloud region at its deepest three-year commitment where hardware is bought. Field Ledger: three-year cost, No hardware line to price: the cost is the integration and software work (Appendix A). Baseline: three-year cost, No compute to price; the acquisition is priced by the survey firm against the operator's own task table (Appendix A). Fodder Watch: three-year cost, About 15,200 dollars to own the node, about two thirds the deepest three-year AWS commitment, and renting would move the data outside the Kingdom (Appendix A). A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan: three-year cost, About 636,000 US dollars to own the frontier node and the site server for three years (a range of 606,000 to 662,000: servers, support and power at the national uniform tariff), against about 773,000 to rent the same eight 141 GB cards and one 48 GB card on the deepest three year commitment in AWS Asia Pacific (Mumbai), so ownership costs about four fifths of it, and about 2.05 million on demand, against which ownership costs a little under a third. Renting is a price reference only, because AWS lists no region in Pakistan and every rented option would carry telemetry and ledgers across the border; the frontier footprint itself stands only when the register verifies a model (Appendix A).

## What ontology or object model does an agriculture and earth observation AI system need?

The object model is the part that makes the system an ontology rather than a pipeline: typed objects for the things in the agriculture and earth observation operation, with properties, status values and typed links. Every published design ships its object model as hyper-ontology/1 JSON that loads into Hyper Ontology. Field Ledger: Farm Site, Field, Crop Cycle, Sensor Device, Sensor Reading, Historical Dataset, Publication, Dashboard User, Access Role, Alert, Report, Data Source Connection. Baseline: Mapping Site, Land Unit, Watercourse, Dam, Flight Mission, 4-Band Aerial Camera System, QL1 LiDAR Sensor System, GNSS/IMU Georeferencing Chain, Multispectral Orthoimagery Product, Classified LAS Point Cloud, Bare-Earth and Highest-Hit DEMs, QA/QC Accuracy Report, Professional Services Agreement, Monthly Itemized Invoice, Contractor Project Manager, Operator Project Manager. Fodder Watch: Farm holding, Centre pivot / cultivated field, Farm enterprise / large farmer, Crop licence (wheat / seasonal fodder), Water source (well) use licence, Green fodder ban control, Agricultural fuel / electricity service condition record, Imagery tasking order, Satellite image capture, Processed imagery product, Restricted-crop detection flag, Violation case file, Field inspector, Restricted-crop classifier. A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan: Barrage, Head regulator, Reach, Gate, Outlet, Main canal, Distributary, Rotation, Shortfall, Season, Indent, Release, Complaint, Crop area, Telemetry reading, Gauge reading.

## Who approves the decisions an AI system makes in agriculture and earth observation?

In every published agriculture and earth observation design a named person makes the decision that changes the physical world; the system prepares it. Field Ledger: Every alert is acknowledged by a named user under a role the operator assigns. Baseline: The operator's project manager accepts each deliverable against the QA/QC accuracy report. Fodder Watch: Every flag is verified on the ground by a named field inspector before a case file opens. A Self-Improving AI System for Canal Irrigation Water Allocation in Pakistan: Agents draft and people decide, by name: the controlling engineer confirms or rejects the drafted cause and correction before any gate instruction is drafted, the indent draft is approved before it goes to the national authority, and the dispute account is drafted from cited records for the officer to send; the gate stays a hand operation on telephone instructions, and every confirmed cause and accepted adjustment is kept as memory the next season reads.
