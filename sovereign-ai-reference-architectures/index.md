# Sovereign AI reference architectures

Canonical: https://codeatoms.ai/sovereign-ai-reference-architectures/
License: CC BY 4.0
Publisher: CodeNinja Atoms (https://codeatoms.ai)
DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.23527131

A sovereign AI reference architecture is a complete design for running AI inside an operation on infrastructure the operator owns or controls, in its own country, with open-weight models and no third-party AI service in the serving path. CodeNinja Atoms has published 21 of them, for 9 physical-world sectors in the United States (11), Pakistan (6), Saudi Arabia (4). Each names its sources, object model, models, compute, three-year cost and the person who approves every action, and each is free to reuse under CC BY 4.0 with a DOI.

## The layers of a sovereign AI reference architecture

Every published design follows the same seven layers, bottom up. Across the 21 designs the object model layer holds 288 objects and 268 typed links, the inference layer names 49 open-weight model choices, and 21 of 21 designs name the person who approves each action. The sovereign boundary is what makes it sovereign: every layer runs on hardware the operator owns or controls, in its own country, with no third-party AI service in the serving path.

| Layer | What it holds |
|---|---|
| 1. Sources | the systems of record the operation already runs, read only; they stay the system of record |
| 2. Sensing | cameras, detectors and instruments, existing or added, sized from the streams they produce |
| 3. Adapters | one family per kind of source, owning its contract, retries and ordering; the only way data enters |
| 4. Object model | the operation as typed objects, properties and links (an ontology), held on infrastructure the operator controls |
| 5. Inference | open-weight models only, placed at the edge, the site or one in-country node by latency and link loss |
| 6. Services | what the design computes over the object model: forecasts, detections, joins, evidence |
| 7. Surfaces | where people see the result and a named person decides; actions are written one way into the owning system |
