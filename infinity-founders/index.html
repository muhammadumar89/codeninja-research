The problem

Intelligence is here. Deployment is the bottleneck.

Frontier AI can already reason about plants, grids, mines, fleets and factories. What it cannot do on its own is walk onto a site, understand the systems of record, and earn the trust of the people who run the operation. If organizations are not helped to transform with AI, the result will be massive job loss, most of all in physical industries. The answer is not one company. It is hundreds of forward deployed engineering companies, each rooted in an industry and a region, taking this intelligence into the field.

The mission

Hundreds of founders. Every organization in the world.

Infinity Founders exists to create the companies that will carry AI into physical operations. We believe this market will be winner take all, and we want the winners to be founders who own their companies and their customers, built on open, sovereign foundations.

What your company sells

Air gapped AI that every organization owns.

Your company helps organizations build their own operational intelligence and keep it. The models run where the data lives, on hardware the customer owns, with no internet connection required. Systems of record are read, never overwritten. Agents draft, named people approve. The customer owns the ontology, the models and the outcomes.
